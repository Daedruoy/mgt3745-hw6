## Trust Boundary

What crossed: the text of prompts/PROMPT.md. That is the five F2 EARS rows, one job statement for a fictional student named Devon, and the style tokens and rules from my HW5 STYLE.md. It went to bolt.new (StackBlitz) under the terms I accepted for my account. I did not read separate data-use terms. No real customer, student, or personal data was sent. I am accountable.

Reverse crossing: the zip brought in a Supabase client (src/lib/supabase.ts) that makes database calls from two environment variables, a migration file, and a project/.env file. I checked the length of the values without printing them, and they were short. It also brought four runtime dependencies (@supabase/supabase-js, lucide-react, react, react-dom), Vite, Tailwind, TypeScript, and lint dev dependencies, and it writes the skills a user marks and the last role to localStorage. A hidden .bolt/prompt file tells the builder to make "fully featured" pages worthy of production with Tailwind and Lucide icons. My prompt never said that. I did not audit telemetry inside the dependencies.
